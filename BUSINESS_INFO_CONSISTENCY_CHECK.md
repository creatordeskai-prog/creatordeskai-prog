# Business Information Consistency Check

Customers can lose confidence when a business publishes conflicting information across its own public surfaces.

## Check these fields

- business name
- phone
- email
- street address
- service area
- business hours
- booking or quote URL
- owner/team names when publicly relevant
- current services
- payment/ordering links

## Where to compare

Start with the surfaces you control:

1. website header/footer
2. contact page
3. booking or checkout pages
4. major service pages
5. public business profiles
6. active social profiles
7. directory/listing pages that customers actually reach

## Verification rule

Do not assume an update propagated everywhere.

After a correction, read each important public surface back and record which ones actually changed.
