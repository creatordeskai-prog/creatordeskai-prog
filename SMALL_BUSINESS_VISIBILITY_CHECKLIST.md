# Small-Business Visibility Fix Checklist

Before buying more ads, posting more content, or redesigning the whole website, verify the customer-facing basics first.

## 10 things to check

1. **Homepage placeholders** — remove Lorem Ipsum, “Add your title here,” “Project name here,” dummy testimonials, builder instructions, and sample contact data.
2. **Contact accuracy** — confirm the public phone, email, address, hours, and service area are current everywhere customers can see them.
3. **Broken contact paths** — test every form, email link, phone link, quote button, booking button, and major CTA.
4. **Wrong-business residue** — look for old brand names, unrelated emails, copied template content, former owners, previous addresses, or another company’s service details.
5. **Google/local consistency** — compare the website against current public business information and fix material conflicts.
6. **Indexed junk pages** — search the domain for old templates, duplicate staging pages, unfinished pages, and abandoned URLs that still surface publicly.
7. **Trust friction** — check testimonials, reviews, team information, licensing/credential claims, and proof for obvious inaccuracies or placeholders.
8. **Mobile conversion path** — verify the main CTA is visible, legible, tappable, and usable on a phone.
9. **Purchase/lead path** — follow the exact journey from search or social → page → CTA → form/checkout → confirmation.
10. **Verify the fix** — after changing anything, read it back from the live public site. A change request is not completion.

> **Automation is not completion. Verification is completion.**
