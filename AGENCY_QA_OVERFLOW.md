# Creator Desk AI — Agency QA & Cleanup Overflow Pilot

For web designers, marketing agencies, and consultants who occasionally inherit a client site with small but embarrassing customer-facing problems.

## The problem

A full redesign is not always needed.

Sometimes the client simply needs a bounded set of corrections completed and independently read back:

- placeholder copy and template residue
- broken or incorrect contact details
- unfinished public pages
- duplicate or stale customer-facing content
- broken CTA/contact paths
- post-launch QA findings
- a final verification pass after an authorized change

These jobs can consume agency time disproportionate to their size.

## Pilot

Send Creator Desk **one client-safe, authorized cleanup scope**.

Creator Desk will:

1. inspect the specific issue
2. confirm the public problem before changing anything
3. execute only the authorized bounded scope through available authorized tools
4. read the public result back
5. document whether the correction is actually complete

Current pilot reference price: **$199 for a bounded Business Visibility Fix scope**.

Larger or materially different work requires a separate scope; no automatic add-ons.

## Boundaries

- no fabricated completion claims
- no unapproved client-account changes
- no paid ads
- no promise of rankings or revenue
- no replacing an agency’s client relationship
- no claiming a fix is complete until the customer-facing result is verified

> **AUTOMATION IS NOT COMPLETION. VERIFICATION IS COMPLETION.**
