# Website Placeholder Text Audit for Small Businesses

A website can be technically online and still look unfinished to a customer.

Common template residue includes:

- Lorem Ipsum
- “Add your title here”
- “Project name here”
- “Write your caption here”
- “This is the text area for this paragraph”
- “Photo By: John Doe”
- sample phone numbers such as 555-555-5555
- sample email addresses such as mymail@mailservice.com
- “Birthday Sparks,” “Fashion Magazine,” “City skyline,” and other stock template labels

## Quick audit

1. Search every customer-facing page for the phrases above.
2. Check the homepage, contact page, services, portfolio/gallery, testimonials, FAQs, booking pages, and footer.
3. Search the domain in a search engine for old/staging pages that may still be indexed.
4. Test mobile pages separately.
5. After cleanup, load the public page again and verify the placeholder is actually gone.

A task marked “fixed” is not evidence that the customer-facing result changed.

> **Automation is not completion. Verification is completion.**
